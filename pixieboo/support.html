Pixieboo Support

Need help covering a video, restoring Pro, or saving a result?

Email support

Purchase help

Open Pixieboo Pro and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Subscriptions can be managed in your App Store account.

Protecting a video

Play the full preview before saving. If a face is missed, add a cover and drag it onto the face. Use Check coverage as an extra review aid, not as a replacement for watching the result.

Privacy

Videos and face information stay on your device. Please avoid emailing a private family video unless you deliberately choose to share it for support.
