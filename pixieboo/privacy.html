Pixieboo Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

Pixieboo helps you cover faces in videos. Your videos, photos, detected face locations, edits, and custom cover images are processed and stored on your device. Pixieboo does not upload them to us.

Information used for purchases

Pixieboo uses Apple’s App Store and RevenueCat to offer and restore Pixieboo Pro. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, purchase history, product information, subscription status, device and app information, and limited diagnostic data needed to provide and analyze purchases. Pixieboo does not ask you to create an account or provide your name to use the app.

Photos and videos

Photo Library access is used only when you choose a video or custom cover and when you save a covered video. Processing happens on your device. Your originals are not changed.

Support

If you email support, we receive the address, message, and information you choose to include. Do not send private family videos unless you deliberately decide to. Support messages are used only to answer your request and maintain the service.

Tracking and advertising

Pixieboo does not contain advertising and does not use your information to track you across apps or websites.

Children

Pixieboo is designed for adults and guardians protecting people shown in videos. It does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Your choices

You can remove Pixieboo’s local projects and custom covers in the app or by deleting the app. You can manage or cancel an Apple subscription in your App Store account. For purchase-data questions, see RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.
