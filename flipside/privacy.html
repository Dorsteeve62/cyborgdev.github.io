Privacy Policy — Flipside

Last updated: August 15, 2026  ·  CyborgDev

Flipside scans food labels and scores them. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with information — because most of the answer is "nothing", and the parts that aren't should be easy to check.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers. Nothing about you is sold, rented, or shared with anyone.

What stays on your phone

All of this is stored on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • Your scan history — every product you have scanned, and its score.
  • Products you have saved.
  • Your preferences — allergens you asked us to flag, how you eat, what you are cutting down on, whether you are scanning for children.
  • A cache of products you have looked at, so the app works without a signal.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. "Reset everything" in Settings does the same without uninstalling.

Photographs of labels

When a product's record is incomplete, Flipside offers to read the label with your camera.

The photograph never leaves your phone. Text recognition runs entirely on the device. The image is not uploaded, not stored, and not sent to us or to anyone else. Only the text read from it is used, and only as described below.

What leaves your phone

1. The barcode you scan

To look a product up, the barcode is sent to:

  • Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), an open food database. Their servers receive the barcode and, as with any internet request, your device's IP address. Their privacy policy governs what they do with it.
  • USDA FoodData Central, a US government food database — reached through our own server, so USDA sees our request rather than your device.

We do not keep a record of which barcodes you scanned. The history is on your phone only.

2. Label readings you contribute — optional, on by default

If "Share labels you photograph" is on in Settings, then when you photograph a panel we could not otherwise read, the figures from it are added to a shared record so the next person who scans that product gets them too.

What is sent:

  • the barcode
  • the nutrition figures on the panel
  • the ingredient list text
  • the date, rounded to the day

What is not sent, and is not stored anywhere in that record:

  • any device identifier
  • any account (there are none)
  • your IP address
  • your location
  • anything else about you or your phone

Because no identifier is stored, two people photographing the same tub produce identical entries. There is no way for us — or for anyone reading the database — to tell which readings came from which person, or to link any two readings together. This is a deliberate design choice, and it is why we cannot offer to delete "your" contributions: after they are made, there is nothing that marks them as yours.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share labels you photograph. Turning it off stops any further contributions immediately. Photographed labels still work — they just fix the product for you alone.

3. Reports you choose to send

If the app hits an error, or if you tap "This looks wrong" on a product, it prepares a report and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it, and it goes wherever you send it.

A crash report contains the error and where in the app it happened. A product report contains the barcode and the label figures we were using. Neither contains anything about you.

4. Your subscription

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. Payment details are never seen by Flipside — we receive only whether a subscription is active. Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction.

Children

Flipside is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The app has a setting for scanning food for children; that preference is stored on the phone and sent nowhere.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, and delete personal information held about you.

We hold none. There are no accounts, no profiles, and nothing in our records that identifies a person. If you would like to remove everything Flipside holds about you, deleting the app does it completely.

If you believe a shared label entry is wrong, tap "This looks wrong" on the product or write to us — we can remove or suppress entries for a barcode.

Changes to this policy

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what leaves your phone will be said plainly in the app before it takes effect.
